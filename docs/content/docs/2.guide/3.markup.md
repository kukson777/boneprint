## What becomes what

- Text becomes one bar per line.
- `img`, `video`, `canvas`, `svg`, `iframe` and form fields become a block.
- A painted element with text, up to about three lines tall, counts as a chip or button and becomes one block.
- Table cells and elements with a divider on one side become bars.

Toggle the switches to see each rule at work:

::code-preview
:markup-demo

#code
```vue
<template>
  <Skeleton :loading>
    <UAvatar src="/emily.png" size="xl" />

    <h4>Emily Johnson</h4>

    <UBadge data-skeleton-skip label="Admin" />

    <p data-skeleton-block>
      Text becomes one bar per line, the avatar and the button become blocks.
    </p>

    <UButton label="Follow" />
  </Skeleton>
</template>
```
::

## Attributes

| Attribute | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `data-skeleton-block` | draw the element as one block |
| `data-skeleton-skip` | leave the element out |

## Your own skeleton

A chart, a logo or a canvas doesn't measure well. Use the `#skeleton` slot to draw your own instead, and build it from `<SkeletonBox>`, which follows the same colors and shimmer as every other skeleton. It takes `width`, `height` and `radius`.

::code-preview
:custom-skeleton-demo

#code
```vue
<template>
  <Skeleton :loading>
    <RevenueChart />

    <template #skeleton>
      <SkeletonBox width="100%" :height="128" />
    </template>
  </Skeleton>
</template>
```
::

## Height

To fill the height of the parent, give the root a height:

::code-preview
:full-height-demo

#code
```vue
<template>
  <div class="flex h-48 flex-col">
    <Skeleton class="min-h-0 flex-1" :loading>
      <div class="h-full overflow-auto">
        <p v-for="row in 12" :key="row">Row {{ row }}</p>
      </div>
    </Skeleton>
  </div>
</template>
```
::

Margins of the content stay inside `<Skeleton>` and don't collapse with its neighbours.

## Side effects in the slot

Slot content mounts once for the skeleton and again for the content. Keep data fetching and subscriptions outside `<Skeleton>`.
